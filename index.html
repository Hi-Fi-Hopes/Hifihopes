    About Hi-Fi Hopes

    Hi-Fi Hopes is a one-person restoration shop in Paulding, Ohio, focused on silverface-era receivers from Marantz, Sansui, Pioneer, Kenwood and Yamaha.

    Every unit gets the same treatment: capacitors screened and replaced where they've drifted, bias and offset set against the service manual, dial lighting restored with warm-white LEDs, and contacts cleaned before anything goes back together. Nothing leaves the bench until it measures right.

    Hi-Fi Hopes, LLC is registered in Ohio.
